The Art of Ying Cha

Honoring Tea Heritage with Modern Flair.

"Ying Cha" (迎茶) translates to welcoming guests with fine tea. We created Ying Cha Café as a bridge between centuries-old East Asian tea ceremonies and contemporary urban café culture.

Whether enjoying a slow gongfu tea tasting session or catching up over espresso and steamed baos, our green teal sanctuary offers room to breathe, savor, and reconnect.
